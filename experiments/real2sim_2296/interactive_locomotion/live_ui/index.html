Controls and simulation details

Press Enter or click Enable controls to play. W looks up, S looks down, A looks left and D looks right. Hold a key for continuous looking. Horizontal looking also turns G1.

↑ walks forward, ↓ walks backward, ← steps left and → steps right, relative to the view. Hold arrow keys for continuous movement; releasing them stops the walking command. V switches between third-person and head view. Q/E also turn left/right.

The pointer stays free during keyboard play. The optional Use mouse look button captures it inside the game; Escape releases it and stops walking. Touch and gamepad controls also remain available.

F walks to the selected visible handle or object, lines up, then performs the shown action: open, close, grasp or place. An arrow key or Escape cancels the approach. Once the interaction sequence starts, it continues until it finishes or a safety limit stops it.

Space stops walking. Looking up/down changes the camera only. During an interaction, body turning is held; move the look control again after G1 returns control to request a turn.

Gamepad: left stick moves, right stick looks, bumpers turn the view, A interacts, B stops.

Leaving the page stops walking input. An interaction continues under its own guards and may pause if a limit is reached.
